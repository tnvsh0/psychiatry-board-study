השלט: "⁨THOS. & CHESS COTS & PRAMS⁩" — Thos. is the standard trade abbreviation of Thomas and THOS. & CHESS letters up as a two-partner family firm exactly the way half a high street does — the Château Tyramine device, where the proprietors' names ARE the chapter's two authors — and the diagnosis word is never spent. The trade was moved off the pet shop because the fixed token is three babies in cribs and a pet shop cannot hold them, whereas this shop's whole floor is cribs with babies being tried in them; and goodness of fit is literally what the shop sells, matching a cot, a pram and a household to each other with a mock car boot and a door-width frame standing on the floor to prove the fit. Temperament to adult personality is the convertible cot-bed shown half made up as a cot and half as a bed, the same article grown, and the nine dimensions are the nine numbered lines on every spec card.
