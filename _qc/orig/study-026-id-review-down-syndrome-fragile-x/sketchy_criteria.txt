The shed's own routine carries the rest on native carriers with no competing meaning: the candling lamp is the trade's radiograph, so a three-yolk egg held up to it is a legal counted set and two bubbles on the same screen is the classic paired sign, while the lot stamp and the numbered size lane are carriers whose real job is to bear digits.
