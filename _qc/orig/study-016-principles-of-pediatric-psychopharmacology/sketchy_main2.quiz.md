השלט: "⁨PHARM LANE KARTS⁩" — PHARM is the bare morpheme of pharmacology on a name that reads as an ordinary rural address ('Pharm Lane'), and the venue supplies the doctrine off stock furniture. The gantry banner 'START LOW · GO SLOW' takes the largest lettering slot, so the biggest string on the page is a principle rather than the venue name. Titration is the throttle restrictor plate a marshal is fitting. Weight-based mg/kg dosing is the paddock weigh-in scale with brass weights, and the height gauge at the gate is the size limit. The black-box warning is the marshal's BLACK FLAG in the flag rack beside the yellow and the chequered — a track's flags already are a graded safety language. Off-label use is the kart whose sponsor decal has been peeled off with a hand-lettered 'NO LABEL' card taped over the bare panel. Monitoring is the pit board 'PIT EVERY 3 LAPS', the growth chart in the first-aid hut and the blood-pressure cuff on its hook.
