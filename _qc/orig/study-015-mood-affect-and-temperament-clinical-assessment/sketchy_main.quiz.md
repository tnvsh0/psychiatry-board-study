השלט: "⁨EUTHYMIC FALLS WEATHER⁩" — The teaching definition already is the picture: affect is the weather, mood is the climate. The venue therefore ships the whole chapter off its own instruments with nothing invented. Cross-sectional versus longitudinal is the barograph — one instantaneous needle reading against the week-long ink trace on the rotating drum, side by side on the same bench. Range of affect is the max/min thermometer, an instrument whose only job is to record a range. Lability is the aneroid barometer faceplate lettered 'STORMY · CHANGE · FAIR' with the needle swinging. Congruence is the observer whose logged figure does not match the instrument he is standing at. Cultural considerations are the wall map with pin flags over other regions, where 'fair' means a different reading. 'Euthymic Falls' is the place-name device — a real-sounding small-town station name that happens to be the baseline mood term, the Chateau Tyramine move.
