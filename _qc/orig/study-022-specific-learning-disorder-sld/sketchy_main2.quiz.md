השלט: "⁨LEXI PRESS⁩" — LEXI is the live morpheme of dyslexia on a name any print shop could really carry, and letterpress is the one ordinary trade in which letters are genuinely mirror-reversed and in which b, d, p and q genuinely get put back in the wrong compartment — the enamel sign over the case, 'MIND YOUR P's AND Q's', is a real typesetter's idiom that exists for precisely that reason. The three specifiers are three benches in one shop with no invention: the type cases for reading, the hand-lettering bench with its cramped blotted practice sheet for written expression, and the spilled numeral drawer with the figures face-up and out of order for mathematics. The hand-lettered window banner 'SPELING GUARANTED' takes the largest lettering slot and spends it on the specifier rather than the name. 'Despite intervention for at least six months' is the job ticket rack labelled '6 MONTHS OF PROOFS', with a stack of red-marked proofs of the same job beneath it. Accommodations are the bench magnifier on its arm and the type-height gauge.
