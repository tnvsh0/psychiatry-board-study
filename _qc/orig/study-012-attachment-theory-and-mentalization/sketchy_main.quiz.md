השלט: "⁨BOWLBY & SONS ATTACHMENTS⁩" — ATTACHMENT is a homonym — the shop literally sells attachments — and 'Bowlby & Sons' is both the theorist's name and the most ordinary ironmonger form there is. Four fittings carded on the pegboard give the four patterns their own prop family (SECURE FIT / AVOIDS DUST / CLINGS ON / TANGLES), the dog lead clipped to the rail outside the open door is the secure base, and mentalization is the shop's real service: a repairman with his ear against a machine working out what is going on inside it, under a card reading WE FIND WHAT'S INSIDE
