## 3. Antidepressants — TCA  (6)

- **Clomipramine** — *Anafranil* — TCA
- **Imipramine** — *Tofranil* — TCA
- **Nortriptyline** — *Pamelor* — TCA (secondary amine)
  - Best-tolerated TCA. Therapeutic window 50-150 ng/ml — narrow.
- **Amitriptyline** — *Elavil* — TCA (tertiary amine)
  - Strong sedation + anticholinergic. Cardiotoxic in OD.
- **Doxepin** — *Silenor* — TCA (tertiary)
  - Low-dose (3-6mg) is pure H1 → FDA-approved for sleep.
- **Desipramine** — *Norpramin* — TCA (secondary)
  - Most NE-selective TCA. Activating.

## 4. Antidepressants — MAOI  (4)

- **Phenelzine** — *Nardil* — MAOI
- **Selegiline (transdermal)** — *Emsam* — MAOI (transdermal)
  - Patch — bypasses gut MAO so 6mg patch = no tyramine restriction. Higher doses need diet.
- **Isocarboxazid** — *Marplan* — MAOI
  - Tyramine restriction. Rarely used.

## 6. Antidepressants — Novel / Rapid-acting  (4)

- **Ketamine (IV/IM)** — *Ketalar (anesthesia branding)* — Anesthetic
  - Off-label for MDD (esketamine = on-label nasal). Rapid onset (hours). Dissociation, BP↑.

## 11. Benzodiazepines  (8)

- **Alprazolam** — *Xanax* — short-acting, high-potency
  - High dependence potential. Inter-dose rebound. Avoid in adolescents.
- **Lorazepam** — *Ativan* — intermediate, no active metabolite
  - Hepatic conjugation only — safer in liver disease + elderly. IV/IM/PO.
- **Clonazepam** — *Klonopin* — long-acting, high-potency
  - Long t½ → less rebound than alprazolam. Often used long-term despite cautions.
- **Diazepam** — *Valium* — long-acting, active metabolites
  - Long active metabolites (desmethyldiazepam). Used in alcohol withdrawal.
- **Midazolam** — *Versed* — ultra-short, parenteral
  - Rapid IM onset → emergency agitation. Water-soluble. Intranasal in pediatrics for seizure.
- **Oxazepam** — *Serax* — short, no active metabolite
  - Like lorazepam — conjugation only, safe in liver disease.
- **Chlordiazepoxide** — *Librium* — long-acting
  - Classic alcohol-withdrawal taper agent (long t½).
- **Temazepam** — *Restoril* — intermediate, hypnotic
  - Bedtime use. Conjugation metabolism.

## 16. Addiction Treatment  (8)

- **Naltrexone** — *Revia, Vivitrol*
- **Acamprosate** — *Campral*
- **Buprenorphine** — *Suboxone, Subutex*
- **Methadone** — *Methadose, Dolophine*
  - Long t½ (24-36h). QTc prolongation. Methadone clinics (federal regulation).
- **Disulfiram** — *Antabuse* — Alcohol aversion
  - Aversion therapy. CYP-2E1/CYP3A4 inhibitor → DDIs. Hepatotoxic.
- **Varenicline** — *Chantix* — Nicotine cessation
  - Reduces craving + blocks nicotine reward. Neuropsych warnings (now removed).
- **Naloxone** — *Narcan, Kloxxado* — Opioid antagonist (overdose reversal)
  - Intranasal/IM/IV. Short t½ — may need re-dose. Precipitates withdrawal.
- **Nicotine Replacement Therapy** — *Patch, Gum, Lozenge* — Nicotine replacement
  - First-line. Combine long-acting (patch) + short-acting (gum) for craving.

## 17. EPS / Movement Disorders Treatment  (6)

- **Benztropine** — *Cogentin* — EPS treatment
  - First-line for acute dystonia (IM 1-2mg). Long t½.
- **Trihexyphenidyl** — *Artane* — EPS treatment
  - Oral. Less sedating than benztropine.
- **Diphenhydramine** — *Benadryl* — EPS
  - IM 25-50mg for dystonia. Sedating.
- **Valbenazine** — *Ingrezza* — Tardive Dyskinesia
  - Newer (2017). Once-daily. Approved for TD.
- **Deutetrabenazine** — *Austedo* — Tardive Dyskinesia / Huntington's
  - BID. Better tolerated than tetrabenazine.
- **Tetrabenazine** — *Xenazine* — Movement disorders
  - Older. Black box: depression/suicidality. CYP2D6 dosing.

## 18. Adrenergic agents (PTSD / akathisia / agitation)  (2)

  - Off-label for PTSD nightmares. Bedtime dosing. First-dose orthostasis.
- **Propranolol** — *Inderal*
